How to Play

  1. 🌼 Dandelion player places one dandelion on any empty cell each round.
  2. 💨 Wind player picks a compass direction — seeds fly from every dandelion in that direction, filling cells until the edge or an occupied cell blocks them.
  3. Each direction can only be used once per game.
  4. The game lasts 7 rounds or until the board is full.
  5. 🌼 Dandelion player wins if the board is completely full at the end.
  6. 💨 Wind player wins if any cell remains empty.

How to Play

  1. Write down all whole numbers from 1 up to the chosen ceiling.
  2. On your turn, claim any number — but only if it has at least one unclaimed divisor remaining.
  3. The Tax Collector immediately takes all remaining unclaimed proper divisors of the number you picked.
  4. When no valid picks remain, the Tax Collector sweeps up all leftover numbers.
  5. Highest total score wins. Beat the Tax Collector!
